# Privacy Policy

## Data Sources and Attribution

### TMDB (The Movie Database)
This application uses the TMDB API but is not endorsed or certified by TMDB. All movie-related data in this application is sourced from TMDB (The Movie Database). We are grateful to TMDB for providing this data through their API service.

For more information about TMDB, please visit: https://www.themoviedb.org

### Data Collection and Usage
- This app collects and stores your movie preferences locally on your device (liked movies and viewing history).
- No personal data is transmitted to external servers except for:
  - API calls to TMDB to fetch movie information
  - Google AdMob for serving advertisements

### Third-Party Services
1. TMDB API
   - Used for fetching movie data, images, and related content
   - Subject to TMDB's terms of service and privacy policy
   - Visit https://www.themoviedb.org/terms-of-use for more information

2. Google AdMob
   - Used for displaying advertisements
   - Subject to Google's privacy policy
   - May collect usage data for ad targeting

### User Rights
- You can clear your locally stored data (liked movies and viewing history) through the app's settings
- No personal data is stored on external servers
- You can opt out of personalized ads through your device settings
